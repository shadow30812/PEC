📖 Power Electronics Converter Derivations & Formulas

1. Single-Phase Rectifiers

ConfigurationAnalytical V_dc FormulaConduction Characteristics
1Φ Half-Wave (Diode)Vm / π ≈ 0.318 VmConduction angle = 180° for R load. High ripple factor (1.21).
1Φ Half-Wave (Controlled)(Vm / 2π) (1 + cos α)With FWD or R load. Firing angle α controllable from 0° to 180°.
1Φ Full-Wave Bridge (Diode)2 Vm / π ≈ 0.636 VmConduction occurs in alternate pairs (D1+D2, D3+D4).
1Φ Full-Wave Controlled(2 Vm / π) cos αContinuous conduction with RL load. Can operate in inverter mode if α > 90°.
1Φ Semi-Converter(Vm / π) (1 + cos α)Operates only in first quadrant; freewheeling clamps output to zero.

3. Harmonic Decomposition & Power Factor

For AC source current in a rectifier with highly inductive load:

• Fundamental RMS current: I_s1 = (2√2 / π) I_dc (Single-phase bridge)

• Total Harmonic Distortion: THD_i = √(I_s^2 - I_s1^2) / I_s1

• Displacement Power Factor: DPF = cos(φ1) ≈ cos(α)

• Total Power Factor: PF = (I_s1 / I_s) × cos(φ1)
